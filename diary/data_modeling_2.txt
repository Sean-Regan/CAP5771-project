Since we are clustering to find outliers in given nutrients/localities, we aren't evaluating our model for bias/variance,
rather we are using it to examine patterns.
Similarly, our model doesn't really have a baseline to compare against since it is built around clustering.

In order for our clusters to capture anomalies independent of regional variance, we need to be able to ensure the model generalizes well to all areas.
Thus, we actually want to fit our regressor with all of our data, so we can pick apart and cluster points that have greater variation in their neighborhoods.
