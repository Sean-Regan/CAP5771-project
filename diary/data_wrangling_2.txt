Due to our main datasets having slightly different product names, we had to run embedding and do semantic matching to find similar products across both datasets. Early iterations of this process used fuzzy matching, but this proved to be much too slow for our large dataset and we had to switch to embedding and semantic matching using faiss instead. However, due to the disparities between the datasets, our WholeFoods dataset only had about satisfactory matches while our Walmart dataset only had about 3000 (much lower compared to the 1400 and 550k entries prior to matching). This will most likely be addressed in the future with trying out different matching models but for this milestone we will focus on getting the entire pipeline and the model working first.
