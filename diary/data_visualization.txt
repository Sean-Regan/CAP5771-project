The main visualizations included were a preliminary data exploration graph of price filtered by product name and brand, and a visualization of the residuals on nutrients obtained from a clustering model. The filtered price graphs allow for a better understanding of the dataset as a whole, which is quite useful as we are approaching this problem through a recommendation lens and the overall view is quite important. The residual visualization is based on expected vs predicted price and those are clustered to show if there's any notable spikes that are difficult to model. In addition, the feature selection widget allows for an exploration of how each feature affects the final clustering and residuals.
