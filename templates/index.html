Clustering Map

How to Read this Map

This map shows generated clusters of nutrients and their associated price information. Points closer to dark red imply the price of that nutrient is closer to the baseline price it was predicted to be. Those closer to blue imply the price of that nutrient is farther from the baseline price it was predicted to be. Note that just because a cluster is a high value, it is not necessarily an indication of price gouging, as sales (which will have negatively high residuals) will also be clustered with a higher number.

Click any point to view its clusters, USDA nutrient ID, and its price.

Technical explanations of each layer
  • Global layer: This uses residuals trained across the entire country, with zip codes taken in as a parameter. This will help you visualize general trends across the country, but not so much anything about individual nutrients.
  • Local Layer: This uses residuals trained in each locality. Since the values are not normalized, unlike the next layer, this is a good way to get a general idea of the spread of how close to its typical value an individual nutrient is priced for its area. View this as a way of viewing general trends in a given area.
  • Local Layer Normalized: This uses residuals trained in each locality that have been min-max normalized. Since the range given to the clustering algorithm is much more constrained, the clusters you see here are much more responsive to slight deviations from their local pricing. This would be what you would want to check for a good indication of a specific nutrient being over (or under) priced.

Grocery Store Price vs Nutrient Analysis

How to Use this Tool

Enter a word or words and a nutrient name as filters for a nutrient to price comparison. Hover over each individual point to see the full product name and zip code.

Why Use this Tool

High-nutrient, healthy food brands can get quite expensive; however, low-cost food brands can be lacking in many vital nutrients. Especially with rising food prices and general cost of living, it is becoming harder to find healthy foods at affordable prices.
